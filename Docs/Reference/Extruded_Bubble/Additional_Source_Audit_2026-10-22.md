## Findings with exact owners and provenance

### A1 | CONFIRMED semantics defect: high Breathing control changes SILENT radius (G22/G23/G13/G14)

- **Source:** `widgets/spotify_visualizer/extruded_bubble_simulation.py` `_reaction_scale(pair,drive,expansion)`: `strength = max(pair)`; `rest = max(0.13,1.0-1.45*strength)`; return `rest + ...` with `drive` and `_lerp(pair,drive)`. Both Big Breathing and Small Pulse call the function.
- **Consequence:** At `drive=0` (settled silence), the quiet endpoint is *not* the sole owner of contraction. With quiet fixed at 0.04, changing high 0.18→0.50→0.85 gives silent nominal radius factors **0.739→0.275→0.130**. Lower-handle sweeps below an unchanged high handle cannot alter the `rest` factor at zero drive. This violates G22's explicit quiet/high-handle-independence acceptance question and plausibly complicates strong/resting/held comparisons. It does NOT prove the operator's musical judder is caused by this function.
- **Action/falsifier:** Keep same IDs, physical base radii, audio and camera; sweep quiet independently while loud fixed, then loud independently while quiet fixed; record `rest`, envelope, `current_radius`, `visual_radius`, projection and real pixels. Define honest independent quiet/crest semantics before any edit. Don't weaken strong peaks, change DSP, overwrite presets, add an oscillator or reset bubble IDs.

- **Reproducer:** A Deep Light/SCENE identity at `(x,y,z)=(0,9.3,0)`, physical radius `0.2`, no pop and full alpha starts fading on a `dt=0.1` synthetic logical capture (`alpha=0.8889`, `leaving=True`) even though the +Y world-margin boundary would be `8+2.25+0.2=10.45`. This is a real source-rule disagreement. Whether it causes a particular visible bubble to vanish **halfway up a card is unproven**; camera projection, source/end, actual Y and Light Type must still be captured.
- **Action/falsifier:** Instrument **separate exit reason codes** for Surface centre-plane, Deep world-boundary, BEAM authored End, contact/Pop, already-leaving, and frustum/opacity. Verify upward/downward/oblique world tracks and native water-plane projection for both light types before deciding which physical boundary should retire each cohort. Do not assert collision or population count without recorded proof.

- **Source:** `advance` retains `_bubbles` across ordinary Settings changes, uses SCENE world's outgoing face on SCENE, and in BEAM iterates **all** nonretiring bubbles against the currently authored BEAM End plane. It does not remember each individual's admission/end policy.

Thus BEAM→SCENE may preserve old groups (already in the lighting handoff), but **SCENE→BEAM also has a distinct accelerated death mechanism**. A switch to Sunlit Surface can separately change lifetime policy for all already-living upward tracks.

### A4 | CONFIRMED separate GPU visibility can make a uniform SCENE geometry look clustered (G07/G09/G21/G27/G32)

- **Source:** `rendering/gl_programs/extruded_bubble_program.py` `_SPECK_GLSL` SCENE uses constant `sceneKeep=clamp(uClouds.y+0.45*uClouds.z,0.35,0.9)` rather than the older BEAM cloud-shaped instance-rejection mask. But `cloudLight`, stable Gaussian brightness and nonlinear endpoint fades still affect luminosity. `PARTICLE_VERTEX` multiplies speck color by `phase = 0.3 + 2.2 * pow(max(dot(normalize(aPoint.xyz-uEye),uSunBelow),0),4)`; this factor alone spans **0.3–2.5 (8.33×)** with camera/light angle. `PARTICLE_FRAGMENT` then uses additive color, a thresholded HDR emission term and `uSpeckBloom`. R45 geometric decoupling therefore does **not** guarantee visually uniform projected brightness. The code is definite; it is **not proof** that the rejected screenshot's islands arise from this rather than actual physical grouping.
- **Action/falsifier:** Capture per-stage native overlays, separately for large/small shell XYZ, per-face speck positions, unlit occupancy, `kept`/`sceneKeep`, `cloudLight`/Gaussian, angle `phase`, source-to-point actual distance, HDR pre-bloom, emission-only, and composited post-bloom. Hold camera/light fixed, then move light only, then orbit camera only. Compare **equal world-space occupancy** and equal physical viewport projected coverage before changing any sampling law. Avoid artificially brightening every particle or blaming counts. This audit should join the lighting-authority repair rather than create another independent emitter.

### A5 | CONFIRMED stacked range compression (G19/G20/G21; interpretation needs operator)

- **Source:** `ExtrudedBubbleSimulation.advance`: quiet activity `idle_rate=0.03+0.06*idle`, `rest_motion_boost=1+0.10*quiet`, `activity=min(1,idle_rate*boost+1.6*level)`; `speed_target` and `drift_target` multiply the authored interpolated ranges by this activity, and `emission_target` multiplies by `max(0.25,activity)`. `speck_amount` **then squares** the resolved emission for instance density (`value²`) and uses `2.4*value` for brightness. Simplified pre-bloom speck energy thus scales approximately with `value³`, before lighting/thresholds.
- **Reproducer:** Using simulation defaults speed `(0.05,0.16)`, drift `(0.3,0.8)`, emission `(0.3,1.0)`, idle `0.5`, collision Off, steady silence 4s: physical stream **0.02970 world units/s**, drift **0.01980**, emission **0.075**, speck instance-density share **0.005625** and brightness **0.18**. Full-band `(1,1,1)` for 8s after silence: speed **1.23363**, drift **0.71082**, emission **0.79127**, density share **0.626105**, brightness **1.89904**. Those are synthetic, **not** the operator's actual authored settings or recorded audio.
- **Interpretation:** Compression is an implemented mapping, not automatically a software error. But it is a concrete independent explanation for nearly ineffective low/quiet-end controls or a dramatic change at loud passages; test actual normalized inputs and rendered px/s before claiming reactivity parity. Distinguish range meaning (physical transport at silence versus within-song music transfer) and respect authored quiet/high controls. Do not fix by simply adding more particles or making the entire scene glare.

### A6 | CONFIRMED disabled-ray pass still executes; cost UNMEASURED (G05/G27/G32)

- **Source:** `rendering/quick/visualizer/implementations/extruded_bubble.py:render` calls `_draw_shafts` unconditionally after probe capture. `_draw_shafts` submits a shaft draw and two blur full-screen passes even when `ray_strength()` returns zero because `rays_enabled=False`. The shaft shader marches 40 world samples/pixel at half resolution; the final backdrop multiplies sampled shafts by `uRays`, so no shafts may be visible even though the intermediate pass ran.
- **Action/falsifier:** On native GL4.6 measure stage GPU timing and pre/post-bloom correctness for disabled rays versus minimum positive rays at P1/P7 and several card sizes/DPRs. Consider skipping passes only if zero-rays all consumers (water/probe, reflections, targets, uniforms) remain pixel- and lifecycle-correct; avoid reallocating every toggle, triggering shader stalls or inserting new presentation authority. This is a **candidate optimization**, never a measured performance win on this host.

### A7 | CONFIRMED ray endpoint semantics do not directly reach authored high (G24/G27)

- **Source:** Simulation clamps `_rays` at `min(0.90, ray_target)`; `ray_strength()` multiplies the interpolated user Ray Strength range by `(0.03 + 0.97*drive)`. For an illustrative range `(0,1)`, a full mathematical ray drive `1.0` yields strength `1.0`, but the current simulation cannot supply >0.9, so even at the ceiling it yields **0.8127**. Many real songs may sit further below that limit. The cap is deliberately documented as preventing pinned rays; lifting it blindly could destroy transients and glare.
- **Action/falsifier:** Trace raw band/onset drivers, `_ray_phrase`, `_ray_lift`, `_rays`, `ray_strength`, shader uniform `uRays` and post-bloom brightness against independent quiet/high handles, including loud-within-loud changes. Decide desired semantics (reachable loud end vs reserved highlight headroom) explicitly before altering the transfer. This is a **source-certain design tension**, not yet a demonstrated defect in native ray responsiveness.
